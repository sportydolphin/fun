### Nov 9, 2026: Leverage index (v1.217.0)

- **Engine.** [`derive/leverage.ts`](../../src/wpbl/derive/leverage.ts): the expected absolute swing
  in win probability over the next plate appearance, divided by the league's average. The outcomes
  from each base-out state are this league's own, from `playRunValues`; the win model prices each
  one at the inning and score in question. A borrowed MLB table is wrong twice here: seven innings,
  and 1.13 runs from nobody on, nobody out.
- **Surfaces.** LI on every play of the win probability chart. pLI and gmLI on the player page's
  Advanced tables, unranked, since high leverage is the spots a player was put in, not a skill. pLI
  and gmLI on the Stats table's Overall side, beside WPA. Glossary entries for LI, pLI and gmLI.
- **Stored seasons.** `wpbl_player_seasons.value` gained `liPa`, `liSum`, `reliefApps` and
  `gmliSum`, kept as sums like every stored figure. `PLAYER_SEASONS_VERSION` went to 3, so readers
  build the boards themselves until the hourly job rewrites the rows.
